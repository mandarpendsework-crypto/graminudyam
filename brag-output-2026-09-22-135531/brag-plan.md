# Brag Plan: FinQuest Reverse Business Finder

## What is this app?
FinQuest is an AI-driven hyper-local business advisory and concessional-credit structuring portal for rural micro-entrepreneurs and SCA field officers.

## The angle
Most credit tools ask rural applicants to arrive with a business idea. FinQuest starts one step earlier: give it the skills, assets, and margin capital already available, and the Reverse Business Finder recommends viable, pre-vetted micro-businesses that can move directly into feasibility, finance, and risk review.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
`What if the first question is not “What loan do you need?” but “What can you build with what you already have?”` Follow the question with the NSFDC/NBCFDC lending barrier and the FinQuest identity.

## Key moments
- Reverse Business Finder: ₹25,000 margin capital plus dairy, processing, and artisanal skills become ranked business matches with skill-fit, budget-fit, investment, and monthly net margin.
- One-click handoff: `Apply to Feasibility Study` turns a recommendation into the Pune / Haveli catchment report.
- Financial confidence: 10:90 equity-to-loan sizing and the 28-quarter seasonal repayment schedule.
- Inclusive safety layer: vernacular voice onboarding plus seasonal stress testing before a borrower is overextended.
- B2B close: verified suppliers, off-takers, pooled assets, and live deployment callout.

## User flow worth showing
Enter available capital and skills/assets -> receive ranked business recommendations -> apply one to feasibility -> inspect catchment demand and competitor density -> structure credit and stress-test repayment -> connect the enterprise to B2B partners.

## Voiceover script
What if the first question is not, “What loan do you need?” but, “What can you build with what you already have?”

For rural applicants under NSFDC and NBCFDC schemes, the barrier is often earlier than the application. They may have skills, land, tools, or a small amount of margin capital, but no clear enterprise to launch.

FinQuest changes the starting point. With twenty-five thousand rupees and a handful of available capabilities, the Reverse Business Finder turns uncertainty into a ranked shortlist.

Select dairy. Processing. Artisanal work. Add the assets already on hand. The engine scores skill fit and budget fit, then returns viable, pre-vetted micro-businesses with estimated investment and monthly net margin. Dairy Farming. Agro-Processing. Bamboo Handicrafts. A recommendation is not the end of the flow: one click applies the choice to a feasibility study.

Now the idea meets the ground. Maharashtra. Pune. Haveli. Demand-gap analytics show seventy-eight percent unmet dairy demand within nine kilometres, sixteen thousand rupees of weekly buying power, and low competitor density.

Then the money becomes legible. Ten percent beneficiary margin capital. Ninety percent channelized credit. A calculator sizes the project, and a twenty-eight-quarter seasonal schedule makes repayment visible.

Inclusion is built in. A beneficiary can speak in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, or English, and the voice assistant captures the profile. The stress simulator tests monsoon dips, reserves, and safe borrowing before risk becomes default.

Finally, FinQuest connects suppliers, buyers, shared logistics, and circular by-products. From “what can I start?” to a matched, stress-tested, deployable rural enterprise. FinQuest. Local intelligence. Institutional confidence.

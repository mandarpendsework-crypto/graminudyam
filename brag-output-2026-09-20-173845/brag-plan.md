# Brag Plan: FinQuest

## What is this app?
FinQuest is an AI-driven hyper-local business advisory and concessional-credit structuring portal for rural micro-entrepreneurs and SCA field officers.

## The angle
A national hackathon pitch delivered at sprint speed: the problem is not a lack of ambition in rural enterprise, it is a missing bridge between local viability and institutional credit. FinQuest turns that bridge into a visible workflow: map the catchment, size the loan, speak the profile, stress the cash flow, and connect the enterprise to a circular buyer-supplier network.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"A viable rural business can still die before its first loan." Cut from the lending-gap statement into the FinQuest portal identity with the Ministry / PS-26091 context.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Pune / Haveli: select the Gram Panchayat and reveal the demand-gap score, catchment radius, and competitor density.
- 10:90 structure: turn margin capital into eligible project cost and 90% channelized credit, then sweep across the quarterly amortization schedule and 28-quarter prudential simulator.
- Vernacular onboarding: the voice assistant speaks in the selected language, captures business and capital, and auto-fills the feasibility form.
- Prudential risk: switch from seasonal cash-flow stress to a safe-borrowing benchmark, showing deficits, DSCR, and reserve logic.
- Circular B2B: connect verified inputs, off-takers, pooled assets, and a local MoU; show risk dropping from 85% to 22%.

## Outro / punchline
"From concessional credit gap to bank-ready rural enterprise." FinQuest. "Local intelligence. Institutional confidence."

## User flow worth showing
Entry on the FinQuest home / scheme directory -> choose Maharashtra / Pune / Haveli / Wagholi and Dairy -> generate the six-pillar feasibility report -> move into Finance for 10:90 sizing, EMI and stress tests -> use vernacular voice onboarding -> finish in Partnership with verified off-takers, suppliers and a generated local MoU.

## Voiceover script
A viable rural business can still die before its first loan.

The gap is not ambition. It is the distance between local reality and institutional confidence.

Meet FinQuest: the hyper-local advisory and concessional-credit portal built for rural entrepreneurs and the officers who help them move.

Start with the ground beneath the business. Maharashtra. Pune. Haveli. Wagholi. A five-to-ten-kilometre catchment, a live demand-gap score, and competitor density that makes the opportunity visible before money moves.

Then structure the money. Ten percent margin capital. Ninety percent channelized credit. A calculator that sizes the project, routes the scheme, and lays out every quarter of repayment, including the moratorium.

Because access is not inclusive if the interface is not. FinQuest lets a beneficiary speak in their language. The voice assistant captures the sector and capital, then auto-fills a simplified profile.

And prudence is not an afterthought. Seasonal cash-flow stress, DSCR, safe-borrowing thresholds, and reserve recommendations show where a business can bend before it breaks.

Finally, the enterprise does not stand alone. Verified input suppliers, off-takers, pooled logistics, shared cold-chain, and circular by-product partnerships bring risk down from eighty-five percent to twenty-two.

FinQuest turns a concessional-credit gap into a bank-ready rural enterprise workflow.

Local intelligence. Institutional confidence. FinQuest.
